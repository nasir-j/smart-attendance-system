User Manual & Deployment FAQs

Mount the camera at eye level (approx. 5.0 - 5.5 ft) near the classroom entrance or lecture podium. Avoid positioning the camera directly pointing towards bright outdoor windows or backlit illumination. Uniform diffuse lighting provides the fastest recognition (< 3 seconds).

As specified in FR-005, any face detected that does not match an active enrolled student with sufficient confidence (≥ 45% / LBPH distance < 70) is identified as "Unknown", marked with a red bounding box, and completely ignored without creating any attendance record.

Duplicates are prevented by a two-layer defense mechanism:
  • Application Logic: The attendance service checks in-memory and in-database whether a student has already been recorded for the active session.
  • Database Constraint (FR-008): A strict SQL UNIQUE(session_id, student_id) constraint guarantees no duplicate attendance records can ever be inserted.

Per FR-010, each student's attendance percentage is calculated using the exact formula:
Attendance % = (Sessions Attended ÷ Total Sessions Held) × 100
Students with attendance ≥ 75% are highlighted as eligible, while those below 75% are flagged for attendance shortage.
